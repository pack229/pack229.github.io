## Six Cub Scout Essentials

Every scout should bring these six essentials:

* Water bottle (labeled)
* Flashlight or headlamp
* Trail snack
* Sun protection (sunscreen, hat, and sunglasses)
* Whistle
* Personal first aid kit

## Shelter & Sleep

* Tent (family-sized)
* Sleeping bag and pillow
* Sleeping pad or air mattress
* Tarp / ground cloth for tent
* Extra blanket

## Clothing

* Sturdy hiking shoes or boots
* Extra socks (2 pairs)
* Layers for cool mornings
* Hat and sunglasses
* Pajamas
* Class A uniform
* Class B shirt
* Extra pair of shoes

## Essentials

* Bug spray
* Lantern
* Toiletries (toothbrush, toilet paper, wash cloth, soap)
* Camp chair
* Wagon for hauling gear
* Trash bag for tent

## Fun & Learning

* Scout books
* Binoculars (optional)
* Camera (optional)
* Favorite stuffy (younger scouts)
